About The Film

THE ORANGE LOOP is an experimental film where all visuals were created using Artificial Intelligence.

While the visuals are AI-generated, the Story, Screenplay, Editing and Original Music are crafted by me, Sai — blending human creativity with AI imagery to create a new kind of cinematic experience.

This film explores what happens when human emotion directs artificial imagination.
