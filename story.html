THE DREAM THAT GAVE ME THE LOOP

I don't know if I should call it a dream or something else. It still gives me chills.

Once I went through lucid dreaming. In that dream, I travelled back in time. I was there, but not really there. I was walking on a street that felt old, like 80s or 90s. I was asking people for the time — "What time is it?" — but no one bothered. They just ignored me, as if I didn't exist.

It felt lonely. Like I was stuck between two worlds.

Then I saw him — a man sitting in an orange vintage car. Something about that color was pulling me. I went to him and asked him for the time.

He didn't answer. He just looked at me. Stared at me as if I had asked him for his kidneys. That look... it was not angry, not kind. It was empty, cold, like he knew something I didn't.

I woke up confused. My heart was racing. Was that a good dream or a bad dream? I still don't know. But it was surely supernatural. It was not a normal dream.

That orange car never left my mind. And that's where THE ORANGE LOOP was born.

Maybe I didn't dream the loop. Maybe the loop dreamed me.
